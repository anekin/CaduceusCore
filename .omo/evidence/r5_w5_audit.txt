## Finding 1 (Minor): TC-SOC1 dual compare is single-sided
====================================================================

Severity: Minor
Blocking: No

The plan's acceptance criteria (line 392) explicitly state:
  "TC-SOC1/SOC2 use dual compare — backdoor read (验证计算) +
   interface read (PCIe TLP 回读, 验证接口通路), 分开记录结果"

TC-SOC1 (test_tc_soc1_pcie_dma_read, lines 222-268) only performs
backdoor SRAM verification. It does NOT independently capture and
compare the incoming CplD TLP payload at the PCIe interface level.

Mitigating factors:
  1. The CplD is constructed by the test itself (send_cpl_for_mrd)
     with known data — the test already trusts its own data.
  2. The full read path (CplD → pcie_dma_wrapper → dma_if_pcie →
     dma_if_axi → AXI crossbar → SRAM) IS exercised and verified
     through the backdoor SRAM comparison.
  3. TC-SOC2 demonstrates the full dual-compare pattern for the write
     direction, proving the methodology works.
  4. The critical risk (data reaching wrong SRAM location or being
     corrupted in transit) is caught by the backdoor comparison.

Recommendation: Add an independent CplD TLP payload readback to TC-SOC1
in Wave 6 before final signoff. Capture the incoming CplD at
pcie_dma_rx_cpl_tlp_* signals and compare payload data against host_data.
