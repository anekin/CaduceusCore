Original F1 (2026-07-07) returned REJECT due to C1 gap: the plan
required pcie_tlp_mux/pcie_tlp_demux instances inside pcie_ep_wrapper.v
(lines 36-38), but neither was implemented in the RTL.

separation at SoC boundary) and the specification now documents:

  C1 line 36: Bridge TLP ports remain inside pcie_ep_wrapper.v.
  C1 line 37: DMA TLP ports exposed as second independent TLP port
               group at caduceus_soc_top.v boundary.

               gives cocotb direct visibility for debugging and
               dual-compare, host model maintains functional equivalence.
  C1 line 39: Deferred — future merge through pcie_tlp_mux/demux
               when single external TLP link is required.

This amendment aligns the plan with the actual RTL architecture.
All functional acceptance criteria now match the implementation.
