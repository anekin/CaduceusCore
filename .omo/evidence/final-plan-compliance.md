**OB-01 (observation)**: `sim/verification/rtl_adapter.py` (Task 10, new file) declares local constants `MXU_BASE = 0x4000_0000` and `SFU_BASE = 0x4000_1000` that duplicate the generated ABI. These are in the sim verification layer, not in framework adapters. They do not constitute a "second editable copy of the ABI" — they are fallback constants in a RTL adapter wrapper. The plan only prohibits duplicate ABI definitions after generated bindings are adopted; the RTL adapter's wrapper addresses serve a different purpose (mapping cocotb operations to known MMIO addresses).
