REVISED CONCLUSION (2026-07-21T12:00:00Z — Phase 9 T4 Branch A Failure Analysis)
========================================================================

CONCLUSION: (B): RISC-V GCC -O2 eliminates or misroutes wrapper MMIO writes
(WRP_K_TILES, WRP_DIM_N, WRP_CMD) due to alias analysis across volatile
pointer casts. Firmware writes intended for MXU wrapper offsets 0x44/0x48/0x3C
land at DMA-space addresses 0x40003000+ instead of MXU-space 0x40000000+.
The wrapper always uses reset defaults (k_tiles=1, n=64), explaining the
K-dependent divergence: for K=128, only 1 of 2 K-tiles is preloaded; for
K=512, 1 of 8; for K=2048, 1 of 32.

Branch A (commenting I/W/O_ADDR at npu_firmware.c:199-201) was a binary
no-op — those writes were already dead-code-eliminated by GCC.

Citation: mxu_soc_wrapper.v:168-220 (WRP_K_TILES/DIM_N register definitions),
          mxu_soc_wrapper.v:186-205 (P9-B fix: derive k_tiles from MXU DIM0)

Proposed fix: Derive k_tiles and N from MXU DIM0/DIM1 MMIO writes (which
the compiler does route correctly) rather than relying on firmware-provided
wrapper registers that the compiler misrouted.
