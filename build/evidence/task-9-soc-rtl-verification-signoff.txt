## RTL probe reality (why pc_if, not pc_id)

- pc_id (IF->ID pipeline register) has NO reset term in this config
  (ResetAll=Lockstep=0 in ibex_top.sv:189) — it keeps its pre-reset value
  through reset and can never satisfy "PC=0".
- pc_if likewise has no reset term; it only moves AFTER release when the
  controller's RESET->BOOT_SET sequence latches the PC_BOOT address.
- cocotb deposits on tb_soc.rst_n reach the DUT with an unpredictable
  multi-cycle lag; a VPI Force on the reset net does not reach the core
  internals. The check therefore asserts the reset by its post-release
  effect (poll for pc_if in the boot ROM entry region within 5000 cycles).
