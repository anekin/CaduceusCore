## RTL finding (documented, NOT fixed — out of scope)

The crossbar accepts ANY master's AR/AW whenever the target slave is free
(m_arready_o = !m_ar_active && (!m_ar_hit || !ar_busy)). If two masters
assert ARVALID/AWVALID in the same slave-free cycle, both are accepted
(m_ar_active/m_aw_active set) but only one is granted; the ungranted master
never receives a response and the arbiter scan (!m_ar_active) permanently
excludes it — phantom-accept deadlock. Reproduced empirically with a
constant-assert stimulus (38 grants, all to master 1; others stuck active).
The TB therefore drives masters one at a time in round-robin order so no two
masters overlap assertion during a slave-free cycle. The crossbar does NOT
claim true concurrent multi-master support. See learnings notepad.
